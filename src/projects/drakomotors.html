The site leans on large-format automotive photography, full-bleed hero imagery, and cinematic video to carry the vehicle's design and performance story — there's very little the copy needs to say that the imagery doesn't already show. Custom scroll-driven interactions and fullpage.js-powered section transitions turn the page into a gallery-like browsing experience, snapping between full-viewport visual moments instead of a conventional scroll.

I was senior developer and project coordinator, leading development and launch on behalf of The Armory Agency's client, Drako Motors. Built on Webflow, I implemented a custom UI/UX layer on top of the designer's WYSIWYG layout, established the dev / staging / production workflow, and built a deployment pipeline with linting and Google Closure Compiler to keep the media-heavy site fast. The site is still live today, now showcasing the Drako Dragon — the platform foundation has persisted through the brand's growth.
