Forms Builder was two products sharing one schema contract: a visual builder where teams designed forms, and a renderer that turned the exported inflated schema into live, themable pages. Angular powered both ends. Next.js and MongoDB owned persistence, collaboration, and the API surface that kept builder and renderer in sync.

The product bet was next-generation forms — friendly controls, a large library of widgets and smart components, mixable layouts and templates, and an early AI assistant that walked users through building polished pages. It was collaborative from the start: multiple people could edit the same form at once instead of handing files around.
