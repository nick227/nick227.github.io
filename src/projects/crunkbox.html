Crunkbox was a community platform for hip-hop and electronic artists, built on PHP, Jamroom, and MySQL. Jamroom gave the site its member/artist-profile foundation — I extended and customized it into a music community hub with band pages, an event calendar, and the tools artists needed to get discovered.

I was the sole developer, which meant everything from the codebase to the community relationships ran through me. The real win wasn't a technical one: we signed up 80 genuinely good artists and started promoting their bands both on the site and at venues around Austin. That work turned into direct relationships with musicians and studios — the kind of thing that only happens when you're building for a scene you're actually part of.
