weareaustin.com ran on a shared PHP CMS built to power dozens of Nexstar Broadcasting local TV affiliate sites from one platform. Each station's site handled news in text and video, classifieds, and advertising, but weather was the traffic driver above everything else — during major weather events, a single market's site could spike into millions of page views in a day, and the platform had to hold up under that load without warning.

I was on the core team that built the shared CMS, from initial architecture through post-launch support across the affiliate network. It was a small team responsible for a platform running dozens of live news sites at once, so most engineering decisions had to account for one station's traffic spike affecting the shared infrastructure every other station was running on.

Tech Stack
PHP MySQL Multi-site CMS Video hosting Weather data feed Ad serving Classifieds module
