South Shore Soldiers is a snowboard camp on the south shore of Lake Tahoe. I freelanced their website — a public face for the camp that covered who they are, how sessions run, and how riders find and sign up for programs around the season.

The brief was straightforward freelance web work: make the camp look like a real operation online, keep the content easy for them to keep current, and ship something that matched the energy of a Tahoe snowboard camp instead of a generic sports template.
