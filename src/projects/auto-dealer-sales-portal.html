The portal onboards a dealer's inventory onto 18 ad and marketplace platforms — from owned storefronts and feedable channels like Google Vehicle Ads and Meta, to assisted listings on Cars.com and CarGurus. A sync engine validates platform readiness, generates feed artifacts with checksums, and runs authoritative snapshots as safe dry-runs before anything goes live. Vehicles that disappear from a feed get reviewed and explicitly marked removed rather than silently dropped, so lifecycle history always records its source.

I built the three-part system end to end: the sync engine as source of truth, an operator console for managing dealers and reviewing readiness, and an internal multi-dealer marketplace that doubles as the platform's own test harness. On top of that sits Performance Intelligence — cached movement benchmarks that show operators whether a vehicle is moving faster or slower than comparable stock, and which platforms are driving observed activity.
