The pipeline starts with a brand brief, and the AI generates batches of platform-appropriate posts from it. A person reviews each batch in a swipe-style approval queue before anything ships. Approved posts go into a scheduling engine that spaces them out based on each platform's engagement data, then publishes to all four platforms through their native APIs.

I built the system end to end — brief ingestion, generation, the approval queue, scheduling, and publishing — and treated human review as a fixed requirement rather than an option to remove later. The posting-schedule model was built from historical engagement data, and each platform integration meant managing its own OAuth flow separately.
