This is an opinionated developer-experience comparison of Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, and Antigravity. They are hard to tell apart, they have all impressed me, and I still pay for the whole roster.

You will get how I score stability, speed, trust, token burn, YOLO endurance, and which one I would keep if I could only keep one.

Stability league

Claude Code was the most stable soonest. That mattered when the rest of the field still felt experimental. Codex and Cursor are right there now. The early lead narrowed.

Antigravity has still shown some hiccups most recently for me. It is also the only one I have not figured out how to put on full YOLO mode. The other three will take a massive prompt, or an even bigger pile of reference documents, and crank for twenty or thirty minutes toward a task. That endurance changed the whole game.

When Cursor first came out, we had to feed prompts individually to drive it. I even wrote a little screen clicker to process a CSV: paste, click, wait a minute, repeat. That era is over. I can confidently give Cursor, Claude, or Codex the CSV and let it process the batch.

Trust vs speed

On one hand, I still trust Claude the most. It is not the fastest. Maybe that is thoroughness. Maybe it is recalculating. Maybe it is taking a break. Whatever it is doing in those pauses, the work usually comes back looking considered.

Cursor has probably shown me the fastest results. I have watched it blast through requests that Codex was hung up on. That speed is real, and it is addictive, until the bill shows up.

My monthly Cursor credit runs out first. I really like when providers keep me on a daily budget. Oversight beats surprise. There is a clean argument for going all-in on one tool instead of four accounts and eighty dollars a month. True. I still like the oversight, and I like having dedicated IDEs for each of them. Different desks for different moods.

If I could only keep one

That choice is tough between Claude and Codex. Right now I would probably go with Codex. It gives me the most feeling of security, and the token spend lasts the longest. Claude is a close second, and maybe better once you factor in Claude Design and Claude Skills. Those extras are not nothing. They change what the tool is for.

Cursor is great and powerful, but it burns tokens too fast for how often I want to lean on it. Antigravity, Google's submission, is rock solid when it behaves, and I am not taking full advantage of the Gemini ecosystem yet. Part of why I got that membership is the Veo credits and the YouTube light subscription. It is a bundle play as much as a coding play.

Current standings

Nobody gets eliminated. They all earn minutes on my machine.

  • Codex — current "if I could only keep one," longest token runway, strong sense of security.
  • Claude Code — most trusted, early stability champ, Skills and Design tip the scale on feature depth.
  • Cursor — often the sprinter, least friendly to my monthly burn rate.
  • Antigravity — solid Google stack entry, still finding its YOLO gear, comes with ecosystem perks I have not fully spent yet.

The funny part is that the products keep converging. Last year's clear winner becomes this month's second place by lunch. So I keep the roster, keep the daily budgets where I can, and let them compete for the next long prompt. May the least hung agent win.
