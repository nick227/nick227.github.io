This is a short opinion piece: after tens of thousands of hours with generative AI, I think it is best at writing code, not emails, spreadsheets, songs, or jokes.

You will get why syntax barriers fell for programmers, why experience still matters beside vibe coding, and why I think this wave was built for engineers first.

Maybe that is just me. I see more value in code than in Excel functions or emails. And honestly, AI is still not a great writer. That shows up fast in songs and jokes.

Code is different. AI has knocked down the syntax barriers. You no longer spend half your day remembering the exact shape of an API or the right flag for a tool you use once a quarter.

We still want experience to be valuable. There is still a gulf of knowledge that vibe coding is not using. Taste, architecture, failure modes, what to leave out. The model will generate a path. It will not always generate the right one.

Even so, we are all vibe coding now. If you are not, you are either writing prototypical code or taking a very long time to get something mature. The people who pretend otherwise are usually slower, not more careful.

The whole world is still buzzing about AI. Little do they know: coders made this for other coders. You guys can try to use it too, I guess.
